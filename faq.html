We offer three pricing models: (1) Fixed-Price for well-defined project scopes with milestone-based payments, (2) Time & Materials for exploratory or evolving engagements billed monthly, and (3) Managed Retainer for ongoing support and continuous improvement programmes. Use our Cost Estimator for a quick ballpark, then schedule a call for a precise proposal.
Our minimum project engagement is typically $15,000 USD for a defined sprint deliverable. For ongoing team augmentation, the minimum is 160 hours/month. Digital marketing retainers start from $5,000/month. There are no upper limits — our largest engagement to date was a $4.2M multi-year programme.

Engagement Process
Our process has five phases: (1) Discovery Call (30 min, free) to understand your needs, (2) Scope & Proposal — delivered within 48 hours, (3) Architecture Review — a detailed technical blueprint reviewed together, (4) Agile Build in 2-week sprints with weekly demos, and (5) Launch & Hypercare — 90-day post-launch support included.
We can typically mobilise a team within 5–7 business days of contract signing. For urgent engagements, we have a fast-track programme that can have engineers onboarded and active within 48 hours for an additional expedite fee.
We use a formal Change Request (CR) process. Any scope addition is documented with impact assessment (cost, timeline) and requires written approval before implementation. We log all CRs in the project Jira board so there's full transparency. Our agile sprints naturally absorb small pivots without needing a formal CR.
